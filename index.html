How it works

NeedForge is a structured membership platform designed around a 10-level model. Each level gives you a clear path to grow, earn and build your network through a monthly membership fee system.

Members enter through a chosen level, complete the monthly fee, unlock the next step and follow their progress from the dashboard. Every active cycle helps you track your products, positions and earnings in one place.

Monthly fee: all fees are charged monthly. This keeps the membership model simple and predictable.
