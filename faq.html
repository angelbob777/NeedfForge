Need to know

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about levels, cycles and account access.

How does the tree work?

Members progress through ten levels, with ten positions shown at each level. Cycle and upgrade values are displayed on the home dashboard.

Are payments processed here?

This prototype does not process payments. A payment provider and verified business rules must be configured before launch.
